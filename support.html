Start with the guide

Review Troubleshoot OBJECT for common navigation, product and cart issues. If you can reproduce the problem, send a request below.

Send a support request

The form accepts one PNG or JPEG up to 5 MB and 20 megapixels. It returns a request reference; keep it for follow-up.

If the form is unavailable, email swirky91@gmail.com with a short subject naming the issue.

Response and escalation policy

  • We acknowledge a new request within one business day and reply within two business days.
  • Business days are Monday–Friday, excluding Swedish public holidays.
  • Critical issues that block browsing, add-to-cart or checkout are triaged immediately during business hours and escalated directly to the support owner.
  • Put CRITICAL at the start of the problem description when shopping or checkout is blocked. If the form is unavailable, use the subject prefix [CRITICAL] OBJECT support.

Coverage

  • OBJECT support is operated by the solo founder of Bikeshed Studios. There is no separate support team and no backup operator.
  • Requests are monitored on business days. Critical requests are prioritised and the owner responds directly.
  • A request received outside business hours, or during a Swedish public holiday, is handled on the next business day.

Prepare a support request

  • Your name, reply address, store domain, OBJECT version and preset.
  • The affected page or product, variant, market and language, when relevant.
  • Your device and browser, steps to reproduce, and what you expected to happen.
  • The exact error, an optional redacted screenshot, and any recent app, theme code or editor changes.

Keep passwords, private preview links, customer information and payment details out of the report.

What theme support covers

Theme support covers built-in OBJECT settings and reproducible theme defects. For a payment service, account or app issue, include the affected Shopify feature or app name so the request can be directed to the right provider. Custom implementation work can be scoped separately.

Privacy and retention

Bikeshed Studios uses your name, reply address, storefront URL, description and optional screenshot only to handle the request. Support mail is retained for up to 90 days after the request closes unless a legal obligation requires longer retention. Ask for removal through the support address.
