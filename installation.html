Install and choose a preset

Start with an unpublished copy and configure the appearance before editing templates.

On this page

Add the theme to Shopify

  1. Obtain an approved Shopify theme ZIP from your preview administrator. If they have already provided a development theme, begin with that theme in the admin.
  2. In Shopify admin, open Online Store → Themes. Under Draft themes, choose Import theme → Upload zip file, select the ZIP, then choose Upload.
  3. Open the new entry in the theme editor. Work on the unpublished theme and use Preview to check it outside the editor.

This guide covers the 0.1.0 development preview. Use the ZIP or preview provided for your store. Shopify’s theme upload and preview instructions

Choose Study or Nocturne

OBJECT Study
Light palette, serif headings, split editorial layout and the Study header treatment. Study is the starting configuration in the current candidate.
OBJECT Nocturne
Dark palette, sans-serif headings and gallery layout.

In the theme editor, use the intended OBJECT preset if available, or ask your preview administrator for the matching configuration. Set typography, layout and palette under Theme settings. The Browser control appearance setting is separate from page and accent colors.

Theme settings define appearance. Section order, images, resource selections and links come from the templates and section settings. Review those separately after selecting a look.

Set your brand and store details

  1. In Theme settings → Brand, enter Brand name and an optional Short descriptor. Add your Logo and Favicon. Keep Demo mode off for a customer-facing storefront.
  2. In Typography and composition and Colors, compare text, button and link legibility on desktop and phone. Recheck contrast after changing any palette color.
  3. Add header and footer menus, assign products and collections in your sections, and replace example copy with your own.

Preview and update safely

Before major edits or a theme update, duplicate your current theme in Online Store → Themes. In the draft, check that the intended templates are assigned to your products and collections. Verify logos, menus, policy links, app blocks and purchase settings before adopting the draft.

Save changes in the editor and test the actual preview URL. Review the purchase checklist in each market you sell in. Shopify’s theme editor guide
