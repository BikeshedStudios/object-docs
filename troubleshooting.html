Troubleshoot OBJECT

Use an unpublished preview to identify which setting or product data needs attention.

On this page

Start in an unpublished preview

Confirm which theme entry, template, preset and market you opened. Save the setting, reload the preview and repeat the action. Use a known published product when comparing two templates. If an issue began after an app install or code edit, record the change and compare it with a duplicate of the last working theme.

Navigation and sections

The Index only shows a Shop link.
Assign a primary menu under Header → Navigation menu and confirm its links are available.
A homepage collection or product is missing.
Select it in the relevant section and check that its publication includes the Online Store.
A scene hotspot is missing or points to the wrong object.
Assign a scene image and product to the block. Check the horizontal and vertical positions and the selected mobile layout. Test the matching product row as well.
Filters are unavailable.
Check Shopify Search & Discovery and use a collection or search with products that match the filter. Review search and filter setup.

Media and products

The gallery keeps the same image after I choose a variant.
Open the selected variant in Shopify and verify which image is attached. If there is no assignment, confirm the available product fallback. Review gallery behavior.
Inspect photo is unavailable.
Switch to an available image. Inspect photo is for images. Video and 3D models use their own controls.
My material, care or delivery block is empty.
Check its value, content or linked page and confirm the correct product template is assigned. For dynamic text, check the key and value of the matching product metafield.

Cart and purchase controls

Quick Buy or Sticky Purchase is missing.
Verify the relevant section toggle, eligibility and the primary product form. Check Sticky Purchase on mobile after scrolling past the form. Review the purchase controls.
The cart shows a stock warning or an uncertain update.
Check the current quantity before retrying. If the drawer offers Refresh cart, use it, then compare with the full cart.
I cannot see pickup or an accelerated payment option.
Check Shopify’s location, inventory and payment setup for the tested product and market. Verify the product buy buttons and relevant section settings.

If these checks leave a reproducible issue, prepare a support request.
